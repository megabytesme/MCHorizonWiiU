## RC 4 Cafe bulk allocations

Patch 0011 reserves a locked 256 MiB Cafe expanded heap at startup for Rust
allocations of 8 MiB and above. The reserved backing is owned by the

starting the resource-table coordinator, which still uses the configured
workers. The desktop load order and gameplay worker settings stay intact.
